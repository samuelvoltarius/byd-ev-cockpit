Panoptes Launcher

Your own home screen for the screen in your car, in the Panoptes style. It runs as a separate app next to the BYD home screen, or as your home screen if you want. It is optional and needs Panoptes.

What can the launcher do?

Pages with tiles

Battery and range, navigation, music, guard, clock and your apps at a glance. You can move and resize tiles.

Bar at the bottom

Home button, Panoptes, your favourite apps, climate, all apps and settings. Always in the same place.

Climate in the bar

Temperature and fan with plus and minus, plus on and off. On some cars it shows heating and cooling levels instead of degrees.

Comfort

Tap “Comfort: seats, steering wheel, defrost”: seat heating and seat ventilation for driver and passenger, steering wheel heating, recirculation or fresh air, defrost front and rear window. Only as far as your car reports it. If Panoptes cannot read a value, it does not guess.

Apps side by side

Spotify and other media apps open next to the launcher. If side by side is not possible, the app opens full screen.

“Map” tab

From 0.1.39: your navigation app next to a status column with battery, range, guard, music and camera. “Start” takes you back to the full launcher while navigation keeps running. If the app cannot be split, a slim status bar sits at the top.

Design it yourself

From 0.1.40 under Settings → Advanced: theme Dark, Light, High contrast or Night, an accent colour, your own background picture with dimming (Automatic, Light or Strong), tile size and tile spacing. You put together the status column next to the “Map” yourself.

Templates

From 0.1.41: “Simple”, “Standard” or “Cockpit”, when setting up and under Settings → Advanced. Plus “Day/night automatic”: the theme follows the position of the sun at the car, without GPS the time of day.

New tiles

From 0.1.40: Lock, Charging and Live camera. From 0.1.41: CarPlay / Android Auto, Tyres, Last trip, Speed and Android widgets. The Music tile also controls CarPlay. If a page is full, a new tile goes on a new page.

Bar over all apps

From 0.1.41, if you want: a small bar with home screen and volume, also over other apps. Plus “Resume music at start”. Both are off by default.

Switch automatically

From 0.1.42, off by default: in D the launcher shows the map, in P the home page, also from the split view. When charging, the charging tile comes to the front.

Patterns and parked clock

From 0.1.42: 10 drawn patterns as background. The “Parked clock” appears large in P when you have not used anything for a while.

Template “Vehicle”

From 0.1.43: a large car, your actual model where available, plus status and music card, window buttons (only when parked) and 4 new scenes.

Design freely

From 0.1.43 you move and drag everything yourself, only when parked.

Bar and controls

From 0.1.43: “Back” and “Recent apps” in the bar, a “Large controls” switch, guided setup with self-test and “Everything back to BYD”.

Home, profiles, speed

From 0.1.43: Home tile (Home Assistant, view only), two driver profiles and a speed limit sign in the Speed tile, when the active Panoptes route provides a limit.

Voice and live surroundings

From 0.1.43, off by default: the “Speak” tile for voice commands without internet and a sketch of detected cars and people around your car, without a camera image.

Music title

From 0.1.43.1 the title also comes without notification access, Panoptes reads it itself. On the BYD the button no longer opens a blocked Android page.

Size

The launcher adapts to your screen automatically. If everything still looks too big or too small, choose Smaller or Larger under “Size” in the settings. This works again from 0.1.40. Rarely used settings are under “Advanced”.

Easy to set up

In the car while parked, in the Panoptes interface under “Set up launcher”, or on your PC in the EV Cockpit from 1.23.

How to install it

You need Panoptes in the car. You get the launcher straight from Panoptes, there is nothing to download yourself.

  1. Open Panoptes. On the car screen: Apps & display → Display.
  2. Install. In the “Panoptes Launcher” card, tap “Install and activate”. Without Wi-Fi, Panoptes asks first whether it may use mobile data.
  3. Open. Afterwards the card shows “Open”. The launcher starts.
  4. Updates. When there is a new version, the card shows “Update”. This also works via Telegram, your phone or the EV Cockpit, only if you allow it.
Installing and removing only work in the car, not remotely, and never while driving.

Back to the BYD screen

  • Home button. The house icon on the far left of the bar always leads back to the BYD home screen. It cannot be removed.
  • As home screen. From 0.1.38.7 there is a switch for this, off by default. If you switch it on, the launcher asks “Use Panoptes Launcher as home screen?”. With “Yes, keep” the Home key opens the launcher, with “No, back to BYD” everything stays as it was. Panoptes never switches anything by itself.
  • Remove. In Panoptes under Apps & display → Display, tap “Remove” in the “Panoptes Launcher” card. Your launcher layout is lost.

What does not work yet

  • Temperature control is not confirmed for every model. Fan and on/off still work.
  • Side by side does not work with every app; it then opens full screen.
  • If a value is missing, it shows a dash. It does not guess.
  • The slim status bar above the map only appears if you allow the launcher to display over other apps. At high screen density “Camera” in it is slightly cut off.
  • The “Map” tab (from 0.1.39) runs in the car but has not yet been tested while driving. How the split screen behaves in reverse gear is still open.
  • 0.1.41 is installed and running in the car. Not all of the new launcher features (Music tile with CarPlay, bar, widgets, templates) have been tried there yet. Still open are mainly the Music tile with CarPlay, the bar over all apps with its volume buttons and whether the car lets you add Android widgets.
  • 0.1.42 is installed and running in the car. Not all of the new features (camera & video in the cluster, switch automatically, patterns and parked clock) have been tried there yet. “Switch automatically” (gear and charging) has so far only been checked in the emulator with simulated vehicle data.
  • 0.1.43 is installed and running in the car. The new features (vehicle home page with window buttons, free launcher and cluster editors, Home tile, driver profiles, live surroundings, voice commands) have not all been tried there yet. Still open there are mainly the vehicle home page with the window buttons, the free editor, Home tile and driver profiles, live surroundings and voice commands.
  • 0.1.43.1 is installed and running in the car (app and launcher). The music title with CarPlay could not be checked there yet, because that needs the Panoptes Launcher as the home screen.
  • The speed limit sign only appears with an active Panoptes route and only when the route provides a limit. Otherwise it stays empty.
  • Voice commands: the speech model (about 40 to 45 MB per language) is not in the app. If it is missing, Panoptes says so. Nothing is downloaded automatically.
  • Whether “Resume music at start” works on every drive is still open. It takes effect when the launcher restarts.
  • Own background picture: whether the file picker in the car shows a USB stick has not been checked yet. If there is no file picker, the launcher says so.
While driving: editing only works while parked (gear P). Video apps only start in P, unless you confirmed the notice “Verstanden, ich entscheide selbst” (I understand, I decide myself) in Panoptes.

Tested mainly on a BYD Dolphin Surf (DiLink 3). On other models some things may be missing or different.
