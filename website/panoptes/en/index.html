Panoptes — your BYD, awake and connected

An app that runs right on your car's screen, plus an interface you open on your phone or PC: a camera guard, remote access, Telegram notifications, its own map with chargers and a link to the route planner.

Important: Panoptes is a private hobby project and not made by BYD. Please read the notes before installing it.

What Panoptes does

🚗

On the car screen

Panoptes runs as its own full-screen app with large buttons. While driving, settings are locked; home, map and guard stay available.

🛡️

Guard with AI detection

While your car is parked, the cameras watch its surroundings. You decide when it records: only on impact, with AI detection everywhere, or only at your own private places such as your garage or driveway. To protect the batteries, the guard can go to sleep.

🌍

Remote access

Reach your car from anywhere via Tailscale. Setup uses a QR code on the car screen and your own Tailscale account.

💬

Telegram notifications

Your own bot reports guard events, charging, a weak starter battery, tyre pressure and updates. Each kind can be switched on its own, with quiet hours for the night. Pictures and videos only if you turn that on.

🗺️

Map with chargers

You choose where the map comes from: the community server (with terrain and free charging spots where operators provide data), OpenStreetMap, or none. Until you choose, the map stays empty.

🔋

“Will it make it?”

With a route from the route planner, Panoptes tells you on the road whether the battery will last to the next stop and suggests a charging stop if needed.

📊

Monthly report

Distance, consumption, charging and costs, standby loss and guard events of a month at a glance, also for printing. Calculated in the car.

📒

Trips and charging

Trips, consumption and charging costs are recorded in the car and shown clearly.

🧭

Route planner link

Connect Panoptes to your account on byd.serveone.at/ev/: show a code in the car, confirm on your phone, done. Disconnect at any time.

🖥️

EV Cockpit on your PC

The EV Cockpit shows Panoptes data on your PC and can change its settings. You confirm the pairing with one tap and a four-digit check number.

⬆️

Updates in the app

If you like, Panoptes looks for new versions itself. Installing only happens while the car is parked with the ignition off.

🌐

Five languages

German, English, French, Italian and Dutch. Home Assistant is coming soon.

How it all fits together

Panoptes runs in the car. Everything else is optional and can be connected and disconnected one by one.

1Panoptes ↔ EV Cockpit (pairing)

You allow the cockpit once with a check number, in Telegram or on the Panoptes page. The cockpit then fetches vehicle data, settings and the trip log straight from the car, over your home network or Tailscale.

What does not flow: remote access, password and ports. None of this goes through our servers; the trip log stays on your PC. The cockpit keeps the access key in your computer's keyring. Anything else it sends to servers (map, 3D model, route planner, usage count) needs its own tick; without a tick, nothing.

2Panoptes ↔ route planner

Once linked, your destination with charging stops goes into the car. Panoptes reports charge level, consumption factor and driving status so the plan is right. If you turn it on (off by default) and fetch your trip log on /ev/, it comes end-to-end encrypted straight from the car to your browser.

What does not flow: the server cannot read the trip log; it only receives encrypted data and, according to the route planner, keeps it in memory for 2 minutes at most. Your approximate position is only sent when Panoptes suggests a new charging stop on the road.

3Panoptes ↔ community map

If you choose it as map source, map tiles and charger data come from byd.serveone.at, with live availability where open data exists. Without a choice Panoptes loads no map.

What does not flow: trips and recordings. The server only sees your IP address and the map area. Shared rules only go to the community server if you share them yourself (off by default).

4Panoptes → Telegram

Your own bot sends you message texts. Pictures and videos only if you explicitly turn that on; they are then stored on Telegram's servers.

What does not flow: no videos on our servers, no detour through the project.

5Panoptes ↔ Home Assistant (soon)

A direct connection between your car and your own Home Assistant is planned.

What does not flow: the data is not meant to be stored with us.

6EV Cockpit ↔ route planner

The cockpit shows the route waiting for you and your charge cards. When it is paired with Panoptes, only Panoptes reports the charge level, so nothing arrives twice.

What does not flow: your trips stay on your PC.

Privacy: what goes where

Principle: your recordings, trips and positions stay in the car. Panoptes does not send camera recordings to the project's servers. Anything that sends data out is off by default; you turn each item on separately, and Panoptes tells you beforehand where the data goes. No ads, no tracking.

What you need

  • A BYD with DiLink 3, 4 or 5 and Android 9 or newer on the car screen.
  • So far Panoptes has mainly been tested on the BYD Dolphin Surf (DiLink 3). On other models some things may be missing or work differently; Panoptes hides what your car cannot do.
  • Storage for camera recordings, ideally a USB stick in the car.
  • A free Tailscale account for remote access and your own Telegram bot for notifications. Panoptes shows you how.
