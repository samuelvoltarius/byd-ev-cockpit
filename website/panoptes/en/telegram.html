Connect Telegram

Panoptes talks to you through your own Telegram bot. You create it once yourself, free and without coding. Then you pair it with a PIN. Done.

What can Telegram do?

Alerts

Guard alarm, charging finished or a charging error, weak starter battery, tyre pressure, new updates, a short report after you park. You switch each kind on or off on its own. There are quiet hours for the night.

Menu with big buttons

Write anything to the bot, for example “Hello”. A menu appears. From then on you just tap buttons.

Check on the car

How is the car? Where is it? How full is the battery? Plus a camera picture and your recordings when you ask for them.

Guard and camping

Wake the guard or let it sleep. Switch camping on or off, only while parked.

Update messages

With “Only notify”, the bot writes “Panoptes … is here” with an “Install now” button and asks once more first. With “Fixes automatically”, small fixes install by themselves once the car has been off for at least 10 minutes, then Panoptes tells you. New features are reported and you decide. Never while driving.

Pictures and videos

Sent automatically only if you switch on “Send pictures and videos to Telegram”. It is off by default; then you get text only.

What deliberately does not work

Through Telegram you cannot unlock or lock the car. Climate, horn, lights, windows and starting or stopping charging do not work either. You can look at pet mode, but not switch it on or off.

What the menu looks like

A sketch, not a real chat. Buttons for commands you switched off are not shown at all.

How to connect it

You need Telegram on your phone and Panoptes in the car. You enter the key in the Panoptes interface, on the car screen or on a PC. Setting up does not work if you signed in on your phone via Telegram.

  1. Open the page. In Panoptes: Rules → Alerts → Telegram alerts. It says “Not set up yet” and shows three steps.
  2. Create a bot. In Telegram, open the chat with @BotFather, Telegram’s official account with the blue tick. Send it /newbot. Enter a name, for example My car. Then a username ending in bot.
  3. Paste the key. BotFather replies with a long key that looks like 123456789:AAH…. Copy it, paste it into Panoptes and tap “Check and save”.
  4. Get the PIN. Tap “Start pairing”. Panoptes shows a 6-digit PIN.
  5. Pair. On your phone, tap “Open in Telegram and tap ‘Start’”. If the link does not work, send the bot /pair and your PIN, for example /pair 123456.
  6. Done. The bot replies “Paired!”. Panoptes notices this by itself. Use “Send a test message” to check that everything arrives.
Tip: Switch on “Reachable while the car is off”. Otherwise your commands only arrive while the car is on.

Is it safe?

  • Only you. It is your own bot. Exactly one Telegram account can be paired. The bot ignores messages from anyone else.
  • PIN. The PIN is valid for 10 minutes and only once. After that you get a new one.
  • Key. The key stays encrypted in the car. Anyone who has it can control your bot. Never share it.
  • You decide. Under “Remote commands” you switch off single commands: guard, camera, location, battery, services, backup. If you send a switched-off command, the bot only tells you it is switched off.
  • Updates. Never while driving. Automatically only if you set updates that way; otherwise only after you confirm.
  • Pictures. If you send pictures or videos, they are stored at Telegram.
  • Unpair. Only in Panoptes, under “Change or remove the bot”: “Pair a different Telegram” only unlinks your account, “Remove bot” also deletes the key. You cannot do this from Telegram itself.
